# Governance

Council is maintained by Kizz. Maintainers decide releases, repository policy,
and accepted changes after reviewing evidence, compatibility, and claim scope.

Major changes to orchestration, advisor identity, provenance, release shape, or
evaluation semantics require an ADR or experiment protocol. Historical
evaluation identities are immutable. New maintainers may be added after
sustained, reviewable contributions and demonstrated care with evidence and
user data.

This governance describes project decisions; it does not claim that Council's
own recommendations govern Kizz or any adopter.
